Inside the council's plan to rebuild the harbor wall

By Ana Ruiz · October 20, 2026 · 6 min read

The harbor wall has protected the town since 1911. Engineers now say a third of it needs rebuilding within five years.

The council's plan, seen by Harbor Daily, sets out three options and their costs, and which streets would close during the works.

Option one keeps the old stones and adds a concrete core. Option two replaces the northern half. Option three moves the wall twenty metres out to sea.

Residents can comment on the plan at the library until November 30.
